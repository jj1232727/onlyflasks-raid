1.

Best in Slot Gear Guide for Arcane Mage

Welcome to our Gearing Page for Arcane Mage. This section will cover everything you need to know about equipping your character correctly.

Upgrading gear in Midnight continues to use the Crest system which lets you increase the item level of gear along tracks that relate to where you obtained the item, which harder content dropping gear that can be upgraded to higher Item Levels. For more information, read up on our dedicated page on how the gearing system works and what has changed in the Midnight expansion.

OverallMythic+Raid

This list contains the highest DPS setup from all sources for Arcane Mage. There are often many items for a single slot that are very close or even identical DPS, especially if you simply shuffle secondary stats around between different slots. Due to this, you should only use the list below as a general guideline of what a set of end-game gear looks like, not to make immediate gearing choices.

This list is by no means a replacement for simming yourself. Depending on your previous Great Vault choices and the gear you have available, your BiS gear might look different in some spots than the below list.

In a few slots, I list items from Mythic+, which can only be obtained at Mythic track from The Great Vault. Generally these items are only best-in-slot at Mythic track, not in their Heroic track variants. You are usually better off using a higher item level piece than the best-in-slot piece if you cannot obtain it at Mythic track.

FAQWhich Tier Set Pieces Do I Want?
In Season 2, Catalyst keeps the item level, stats and any special bonuses of an item, so it does not matter which 4 out of 5 slots you catalyze. Pick any 4 out of 5 you have available from the list below:
  • Head — Venomkeeper's Horrific Cowl
  • Shoulders — Ornaments of the Eternal Coil
  • Chest — Primordial Robe of Rites
  • Hands — Grasps of the Eternal Shadow
  • Legs — Initiate's Sacrificial Tights

Note: getting 4-set in any capacity is much more important than getting them on the correct (catalyzed) pieces, so always prioritize getting 4-set over the right pieces.

FAQWhat Items From the Great Vault Should I Pick?
Generally, you will want to focus on picking items that are the biggest upgrade for you by simming Top Gear. However, there are also some items in general that are very valuable and almost always worth picking up if you see them:
  • Jan'thrazet, the Soul Fang: Best-in-slot Weapon by a large margin and very rare and wanted by all casters, so always pick this up if it is in your vault in a Mythic slot (9/6 Myth track)
  • Any of the other 9/6 Myth track items listed below: All of them are best-in-slot for both Arcane Mage and generally all other cloth wearers, so very difficult to obtain otherwise.
  • Freightrunner's Flask: Best-in-slot Trinket which can only be obtained at Myth track from The Great Vault.

Trinket Recommendations for Arcane Mage in Season 2

There are a great number of Trinkets available for Arcane. Generally, you will want to use a strong on-use Trinket to pair it with Arcane Surge, and then a strong passive Trinket as your second Trinket.

Aim to pick up one trinket from each of these categories as a pair:

Arcane Mage Trinkets On-Use - Freightrunner's Flask, or, alternatively Vile Vial of Volatile Venom (slightly weaker)
Passive - Gebbo's Bottomless Bag, or, alternatively Wavecaller's Seastone (slightly weaker)

There are quite a few other valuable trinkets out there, so make sure to click on our ranking chart to see if they are worth equipping.

3.

Nebulous Voidcores (Bonus Rolls)

Nebulous Voidcore can be used to guarantee items from a specific boss or dungeon. Each time you use it, you are guaranteed to obtain one item from that boss or dungeon, and that item is then removed from the pool of possible items. Then the next time you use it, you are guaranteed any of the remaining items, which means that you will obtain the item you are after from that boss or dungeon within a few uses.

3.1.

Recommended Nebulous Voidcore Usage for Arcane Mage

The best items to chase after depend entirely on what items you have already obtained so far, and which content you are clearing. For example, if you have not yet cleared a specific boss on Mythic, but expect to do so soon, you should not use the Voidcore on the Heroic version of that boss. Below I list a generic priority list of Voidcore items, but for the best results tailored to your own character, you should use Raidbots Droptimizer.

Voidcore (Bonus Roll) Priority
  1. Mythic Jan'thrazet, the Soul Fang — Best Weapon, by a large margin, due to its higher item level. Ula'tek also drops the next two items below, making it a very valuable bonus roll boss.
  2. Mythic Venomkeeper's Horrific Cowl — Best helm due to its higher item level and Venomcursed bonus effect.
  3. Mythic Aqirbane Reliquary — Best neck due to its higher item level and Venomcursed bonus effect.
  4. Any Myth Track Weapon — None of them will beat Jan'thrazet, the Soul Fang from Ula'tek, but if you are not planning / expecting to kill Mythic Ulatek, getting a Myth track weapon from other sources is important, especially if you did not craft one.
  5. Higher item level items from Coiled Altar, especially Grasps of the Eternal Shadow.
  6. Remaining BiS List items
4.

Best Crafted Items for Arcane Mage

What to craft is highly dependent on your own personal goals, what content you are doing, and what items you have looted so far. We strongly suggest you run your own simulations on raidbots by using droptimizer to figure out your personal best crafting options.

4.1.

Best Embellishments for Arcane Mage

Below we list the best crafted Embellished items, but make sure to check the crafting order below for more context, since the best Embellishments are not always the best to craft first!

  • Aln'hara Lantern with Hunter's Ritual Stone
  • Martyr's Bindings with Arcanoweave Lining
4.2.

Crafting Order for Arcane Mage

As your ability to craft items will be time-gated in the initial stages of the season, we highly recommend getting a Weapon as soon as possible, unless you can reliably get one elsewhere. Once you have a good weapon, work on your other Embellished Item next, and then craft whichever pieces you do not have high-item-level versions of yet.

Eventually, you will re-craft (or fully replace) the Weapon so that it is not Embellished anymore to open up a slot for a different Embellished item, but only after upgrading low item level pieces.

Arcane Mage Crafted Gear Priority
  1. Aln'hara Cane with Hunter's Ritual Stone: Getting a weapon craft early provides an immense amount of power. If you expect to get a Myth track weapon early, you can skip this.
  2. Martyr's Bindings with Arcanoweave Lining: Can also craft any other slot, the important part is getting Arcanoweave Lining on a piece of gear, and replacing a low item level piece. Wrist is best-in-slot, but you can re-craft later anyway.
  3. Any item you do not have on Myth track yet.
  4. When possible, you will want to replace the crafted Aln'hara Cane with a higher item-level 1-handed Myth track weapon without an Embellishment. Then, you can craft Aln'hara Lantern with Hunter's Ritual Stone as your second Embellishment.

For more information on how you can craft your own gear, you can visit our Professions page.

4.3.

Adding Sockets to Gear

You can add sockets to Helmets, Belts, and Bracers with the Miasmic Jewelbinder consumable that you can get if you do not select any of the choices in your Great Vault. Not picking a choice will grant you tokens, six of which can be traded in for the Miasmic Jewelbinder item that allows you to add a socket to the above piece of gear, if you acquired them in Season 2.

5.

Crest Usage

Early into the season, or if you have just started playing an alt, you will have limited access to upgrading-crests such as Myth Mistcrest. These can be used to upgrade gear to higher item levels. Since crests are limited each week and upgrading different items costs the same amount of crests regardless, you will want to prioritize your usage of them on items you plan on not replacing anytime soon, or that offer the biggest benefits.

Arcane Mage Crest Upgrade Priority Weapon (Staff to save crests) ➜ Trinkets ➜ Helmet/Chest/Legs ➜ Shoulders/Gloves/Belt/Boots

This order is determined by the power you get from upgrading these slots. Of course, which is best to upgrade can be heavily influenced based on what you loot, as you can avoid using Crests altogether by looting higher item level items in those slots, so make sure that you do not blindly follow the provided upgrade order!

6.

Evaluating Upgrades

Throughout this page and elsewhere in our guide, we regularly refer to the importance of simming yourself on Raidbots. This is because every character is unique, and each gear piece you have access to can change what is best right now for you.

While we do offer recommendations for gear and trinkets, these are not complete replacements for simming yourself. The only way to truly optimize your character to that degree is by making use of the tools available!
